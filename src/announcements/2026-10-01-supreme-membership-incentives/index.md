<h2>Membership / Food for Families</h2>
<p>Councils that fulfill their membership quota and participate in the Food for Families program by <strong>November 30, 2026</strong> receive eight sets of custom embroidered Knights of Columbus aprons and hats. Councils also earn a chance to win an additional <strong>$200 Knights Gear gift certificate</strong>.</p>
<h2>Go for 2 More Rewards</h2>
<p>Individual Knights who get <strong>two men to join between September 1 and December 31, 2026</strong> will be entered in a drawing for <strong>$1,500 cash</strong>. Ten second-place winners receive customized Knights of Columbus branded items.</p>
<p>Invite <strong>four men</strong> and be entered into the drawing twice.</p>
<p>Both incentives are for insurance jurisdictions only; other restrictions apply, as stated on the flyer.</p>
<h2>District Deputy quarterly incentives for 2026–27</h2>
<p>Supreme offers a new District Deputy incentive each quarter of the 2026–27 fraternal year. <strong>Only District Deputies from insurance jurisdictions are eligible for these awards.</strong></p>
<h3>First quarter: July 1–September 30, 2026</h3>
<p>Attain <strong>35% or better of the District Intake Goal by September 30, 2026</strong> to receive a <strong>padfolio</strong>.</p>
<h3>Second quarter: October 1–December 31, 2026</h3>
<p>Attain <strong>75% or better of the District Intake Goal by December 31, 2026</strong>, with <strong>50% of councils recruiting one or more new members or exemplifying one</strong>, to receive a <strong>pickleball set</strong>.</p>
<h3>Third quarter: January 1–March 31, 2027</h3>
<p>Attain <strong>110% or more of the District Intake Goal by March 31, 2027</strong>, with <strong>75% of councils recruiting one or more new members or exemplifying one</strong>, to receive a <strong>shirt and tie</strong>.</p>
<h3>Fourth quarter: April 1–June 30, 2027</h3>
<p>Attain <strong>Star District</strong>, with <strong>all councils gaining at least one new member</strong>, to receive a <strong>Solo Stove Mesa Smokeless Fire Pit</strong>.</p>
