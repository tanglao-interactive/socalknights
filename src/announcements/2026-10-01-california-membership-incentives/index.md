<h2>First quarter incentive</h2>
<p>Sign up <strong>three members by the end of the first quarter</strong> to earn a <strong>Star Recruiter baseball cap</strong>.</p>
<h2>Second quarter incentives</h2>
<ul>
<li>Sign up <strong>six members by the end of the second quarter</strong> to earn a <strong>hat and polo</strong>.</li>
<li>Sign up <strong>ten members by the end of the second quarter</strong> to earn a <strong>hat, polo, and jacket</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>The flyer presents these rewards as part of the California State Council’s 2026–27 Membership Incentive Program.</p>
