- **Leadership titles:** Prefix officer titles with “Chapter,” such as “Chapter President.”
- **General email:** None. Do not publish a general email address or nonfunctional contact form.

- **Regular business meeting:** Second Friday of each month at 7:00 p.m.
- **Executive Officers planning meeting:** Monday before the second Friday of each month, held on Zoom.
- **Meeting venue:** Forthcoming. St. Vincent de Paul Hall is being considered, but the location may rotate and is not approved for publication.

- Districts 94–114 are exactly 21 districts, but their rows contain **74 council assignments**.
- The workbook also contains **District 173**, with nine additional council numbers. Including that block produces **22 districts and 83 listed council assignments**.

## Recommended Contact Policy

For the public MVP, display each District Deputy’s name but do not publish the workbook’s home address, membership number, spouse name, personal phone number, or personal email address without explicit consent.

Recommended options after approval:

1. Create Chapter-managed forwarding addresses, such as `district94@socalknights.org`, which can forward privately to each District Deputy.
2. If forwarding addresses are not available, publish a District Deputy’s personal email only after that person expressly approves it.
3. Keep phone numbers private unless a District Deputy specifically asks for a public phone listing.
